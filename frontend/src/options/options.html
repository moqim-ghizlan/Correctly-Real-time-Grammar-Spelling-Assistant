Connection test

This test sends a short prompt to Gemini and may use quota or incur charges. It sends no text from websites.

Correction

How to use: select text on a web page (up to 2,000 characters), then click the Correctly toolbar button. Only the selection is sent, only on that click. Nothing changes until you click Apply, which is available in plain text boxes; elsewhere, copy the suggestion. Password, email, phone, URL, number and search fields are never read. Each correction counts toward the daily limit below and may incur Gemini charges. Suggestions can be wrong: check them.

Correction shortcuts & entry points

AI is called only after you activate Correctly. Each call may count against your Gemini usage. The toolbar button always works.

Some key combinations are reserved by the browser or other apps.

Rewrite

Styles selected by default

You can change the styles each time before generating. All chosen styles are produced by one Gemini request (one request in the daily limit), in the language of your text — no translation. Rewrites need an internet connection and may incur charges; they are suggestions to review, and nothing changes until you click Apply.
