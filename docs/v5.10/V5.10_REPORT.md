## Security assessment

All the static and automated checks listed in
[V5.10_AUDIT.md §4](V5.10_AUDIT.md) pass:

- keys: never in replies, the DOM, IndexedDB, exports or `dist/`;
- forged messages and arbitrary URLs: refused;
- no remote code, no redirect, two fixed provider origins.

Residual risks R-1 to R-4 are documented there. **Not run**: a real
content-script probe of `storage.local` in Chrome/Brave (SEC-016/017) and a
2-minute idle typing capture (SEC-020).

## Quota/accounting assessment

- **Last slot**: 20 simultaneous requests for 1 slot in real Chrome and
  Brave → 1 sent, 19 refused locally (L1). In tests: 10 requests from two
  worker instances for 3 slots → exactly 3 calls.
- **Restart**: the worker was stopped in the browser and the count persisted
  (M9). In tests, a reservation that was never sent is given back, and a sent
  request becomes "unknown", never replayed.
- **80/95/100**: the 5,000-cap example and the cap-of-1 case are covered in
  `quota-store.test.ts`. Each warning appears once.
- **Fail closed**: a corrupted policy or no IndexedDB means no request.
  Deleting statistics frees nothing.

## Rollback plan

Keep the V5.9 folder (or tag `v5.9.0`). V5.9 reads the same data. Do not go
back to V5.8 or older (IndexedDB v1): see [USER_GUIDE.md §7](USER_GUIDE.md).
Export your shortcuts first. Keys must be re-entered after an uninstall.

## Final decision

**NO-GO for the time being.** The release candidate is ready and no P0, P1 or
P2 defect is known. However, spec §23 requires evidence that:

- the keyboard and right-click triggers work in Chrome **and** Brave;
- a bounded live call works with a real key.

Neither could be produced without the owner, so the GO criteria are not met.
It becomes **GO** when the owner completes
[MANUAL_CHECKLIST.md](MANUAL_CHECKLIST.md) sections A–C with PASS in both
browsers, and records the results here.
