## Rules implemented

### Counting

- One generation request counts once, whatever its styles or languages.
  Translate & rewrite counts once, in its own category.
- A confirmed **Test** counts in the total, provider and key, never in an
  action cap.
- Key checks and text shortcuts never count.

### Limits

- Each limit is an intersection: total, action, provider and key, each by
  day, week and month. Every limit that is on must allow the request.
- The twelve counters of a request (total, action, provider and key × day,
  week and month) are always updated, even for limits that are off. So
  turning a limit on enforces the usage already made.
- At least one total limit must stay on. Pause is the way to stop everything.
- Defaults:
  - The V5.1–V5.7 daily limit (20 by default) becomes the total daily limit.
  - All other limits are off and pre-filled with ×7 (weekly) and ×30
    (monthly).

### Request lifecycle

1. **Admit**: an atomic reservation.
2. **Re-check before sending**:
   - pause, clock and limits as saved now;
   - a day, week or month that has started moves the reservation into the
     new windows, which are checked again.
3. **Mark sent**: counted for good, warnings recorded in the same transaction.
4. **Send once**.
5. **Settle**: completed, failed or unknown. Nothing is refunded once marked
   sent.
6. **Release**: only an attempt never marked sent is given back, exactly once.

### Recovery after a stopped worker

- An attempt reserved by a dead worker but never marked sent is given back.
- An attempt marked sent becomes **unknown**: it stays counted and is never
  replayed.
- The live worker's own requests (same `bootId`) are never touched.

### Failure, clock and time zone

- **Fail closed.** No IndexedDB, damaged limits, a negative counter, or an
  import marker without limits: nothing is sent. The settings offer **Restore
  safe limits**, which keeps usage.
- **Clock.** A clock moved back more than 5 minutes blocks requests until
  confirmed.
- **Time-zone change.** It carries the current day, week and month counts
  into the new windows (conservative). Nothing is reset.

### Warnings

- One notice per limit, period, window and level (80 / 95 / 100 %). Each
  identity is stored, so reopening the page, restarting the worker or
  raising the limit never replays it.
- One small-cap event shows only its highest level, and simultaneous
  crossings make one notice.
- Notes shown in pages carry fixed labels and numbers only: no key label,
  key or text.

- A request that was sent but failed (provider error, timeout) still
  returns the warning it crossed: it is counted, so the page shows it too.

### Concurrency

- Settings saves compare the revision. A stale tab gets "changed in another
  tab or window" and a reload button.

### Migration

- Today's V5.7 count is imported once, atomically and idempotently, into the
  current day, week and month.
- Attributed routes go to their action, provider and key. The rest counts in
  the total only.
- The old `correctly.v5.usage` and `settings.safety` are left untouched and
  unused. Keys, routes, shortcuts and preferences are not touched.

### Retention

- Counters are kept (for V5.9).
- Finished operations are pruned after 90 days, and alerts 40 days after
  their window ends.

  - other apps using the same keys are not seen;
  - weekly and monthly usage before the upgrade day is unknown (V5.7 kept one
